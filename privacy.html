Privacy Policy

Muuka is an app for keeping track of the bills you pay. This page explains what it stores and what it does not.

What Muuka stores

The bills, amounts, due days, categories and payment dates you enter, so the app can show them back to you. They are kept in a database hosted by Supabase and are tied to your account.

Your account is either anonymous, created automatically on your device, or linked to Sign in with Apple if you choose to sign in. With Sign in with Apple, Muuka receives an identifier from Apple and, only if you agree to share it, your email address. Muuka does not use your email for anything other than identifying your account.

What Muuka does not do

  • It does not connect to your bank and cannot make payments.
  • It does not collect your name, address, contacts, location or payment details.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking, and it does not share your data with anyone.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are never sent to a server.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and every record in it at any time from Settings, under Account, Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
