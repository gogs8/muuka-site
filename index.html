Support

Muuka keeps track of the bills you pay each month. It is a record kept by you. It does not connect to a bank and cannot pay anything.

Get in touch

Email leelo.studio@gmail.com with a question, a problem or an idea. A short description of what you did and what you saw helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I mark a bill as paid?
On Home, swipe the bill to the right, or tap it. Confirm the amount and it moves to Paid.
I marked the wrong bill. How do I undo it?
Open Paid, tap the bill, and choose “Mark as unpaid”.
A bill does not apply this month.
Tap the bill on Home and choose “I don't have to pay this month”. It comes back next month.
How do I stop tracking a bill without losing its history?
Hold the bill to edit it, then choose “Stop tracking”. You can bring it back from Settings.
Will I lose my bills if I change phones?
Not if you sign in. Open Settings, Account, and use Sign in with Apple. Your bills then follow your Apple account.
How do I delete everything?
Settings, Account, Delete account. This removes every bill, payment and plan for good.
