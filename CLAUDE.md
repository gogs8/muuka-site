# muuka-site

The public support and privacy pages for Muuka, the iOS bill tracker
(app repo: `gogs8/muuka`). Two static pages plus one stylesheet, published by
GitHub Pages at https://gogs8.github.io/muuka-site/ — every push to `main`
goes live within a minute.

- `index.html` — Support (what the app does, contact leelo.studio@gmail.com).
- `privacy.html` — Privacy policy. Its URL is on the App Store listing; keep it truthful
  to what the app stores (bills, payments, an anonymous or Apple-signed account).
- `style.css` — Muuka's design language: warm paper background, ink text, Manrope,
  rounded cards. Keep the look in step with the app.

## How we work

- Goran (designer) reviews every change in the browser before it is pushed; give him
  one step at a time when he has to do something himself, and wait for "done".
- Plain HTML/CSS, no build step, no framework, no tracking scripts.
- Croatian or English in chat, whichever he uses. The pages stay in English.
